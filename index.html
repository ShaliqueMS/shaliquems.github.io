01 / About

Research with
people at its centre.

I am Shalique M.S., an Assistant Professor of Production & Operations Management at the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore.

My research sits at the intersection of behavioural science, sustainability and operations management. Using laboratory and field experiments, causal inference and data-driven methods, I investigate why people and organisations make the choices they do—and what can make those decisions better.

I am particularly interested in sustainable supplier development, organisational responses to ESG incentives, decision-making under uncertainty and collaboration between humans and artificial intelligence. My work connects academic research with agriculture, business and public policy.

02 / Research

Questions that matter.

An interdisciplinary research agenda spanning individuals, organisations and supply chains.

RESEARCH AREA 01

Sustainable choices in agricultural supply chains

What shapes farmers' and suppliers' choices when the benefits of sustainable practices are uncertain or delayed? My field and experimental work examines time preferences, risk, and behavioural barriers to participation in sustainable value chains.

Human–AI collaboration

How do people respond to algorithmic advice in operational decisions? I study trust, overrides, and behavioural interventions in human–AI collaboration, including supply chain contexts.
